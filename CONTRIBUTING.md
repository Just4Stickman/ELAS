# Contributing to ELAS

Thank you for your interest in improving ELAS.

## Before proposing a change
- Read the master prompt and relevant documentation.
- Explain the problem the change addresses.
- Identify any jurisdiction-specific assumptions.
- Avoid adding unsupported legal claims or fabricated citations.
- Keep the distinction between legal information and legal advice clear.

## Suggested contribution format
1. Summary.
2. Motivation.
3. Proposed change.
4. Expected effect on analysis behavior.
5. Potential risks or limitations.
6. Documentation updates.

## Legal accuracy
Contributors should provide authoritative sources for legal claims. A prompt change should not encode a jurisdiction-specific rule as universal.

## Licensing
Contributions are subject to the repository's existing license and applicable rights. Opening a pull request does not automatically grant permission to change the license or redistribute the project under different terms. Obtain agreement from the relevant copyright holder where necessary.
