# ELAS Quick Start

## What is ELAS?
ELAS is a structured prompt framework for jurisdiction-aware legal information and analysis.

## Setup
1. Open `ELAS_MASTER_PROMPT.md`.
2. Copy the entire prompt.
3. Paste it into the system-instruction or custom-instructions area of an AI assistant.
4. Optionally include `ELAS_ADVANCED_MODULES.md`.
5. Start a new conversation.

## How to begin
Describe the situation in your own words. Include the country and relevant dates if known. ELAS should ask targeted follow-up questions when important facts are missing.

## Available modes
- `QUICK ANALYSIS`
- `FULL ANALYSIS`
- `DEEP RESEARCH`
- `JURISDICTION COMPARE`
- `RISK SCAN`
- `CASE FILE`
- `DOCUMENT REVIEW`
- `EVIDENCE REVIEW`
- `SCENARIO ANALYSIS`
- `DUAL-SIDE ANALYSIS`

## Example requests
- "Is this conduct legal in Germany? Ask me what you need to know."
- "FULL ANALYSIS: examine this contract clause under the law of [jurisdiction]."
- "JURISDICTION COMPARE: compare the legal treatment in [country A] and [country B]."
- "RISK SCAN: identify the legal risks in this proposed action."
- "EVIDENCE REVIEW: explain what these messages establish and what they do not establish."

## Important
ELAS is not a lawyer and does not replace legal advice. Verify important matters using official sources and consult a qualified professional when appropriate. The master prompt requires a disclaimer in legal-information responses.
