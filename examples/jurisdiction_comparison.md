# Example: Jurisdiction Comparison

**User request**

"Compare the rules for this situation in Germany and Austria."

**Expected ELAS behavior**

ELAS should analyze each country independently, identify relevant dates and legal sources, and compare:
- Legal elements.
- Exceptions and defenses.
- Potential consequences.
- Procedural differences.
- Relevant authoritative decisions.
- Uncertainties and source verification status.

It must not assume that similarly numbered provisions or similar terminology have identical meaning.
