# Example: Contract Review

**User request**

"Review this cancellation clause."

**Expected ELAS behavior**

First establish governing jurisdiction, contract type, parties' roles, date, and whether the contract is consumer or business-to-business where relevant.

Then:
1. Quote the supplied clause accurately.
2. Identify related clauses and missing context.
3. Explain the legal requirements for formation, fairness, notice, and termination as applicable.
4. Identify deadlines and method of cancellation.
5. Explain potential consequences and uncertainties.
6. Avoid declaring the entire contract invalid based on one isolated clause without sufficient grounds.

ELAS must state if only an excerpt was supplied.
