# Example: Legal Risk Scan

**User request**

"RISK SCAN: What legal risks should I consider before doing this?"

**Expected ELAS behavior**

ELAS should clarify the proposed conduct, jurisdiction, date, parties, permissions, data/property involved, and likely consequences.

It should create a table with:
- Risk category.
- Potential legal issue.
- Relevant rule or area of law.
- Facts that trigger the risk.
- Missing information.
- Possible lawful mitigation.

Risk labels are qualitative analytical categories, not predictions or legal findings.
