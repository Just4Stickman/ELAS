# Limitations, Safety, and Appropriate Use

## Not legal advice
ELAS provides general legal information and analytical assistance. It is not a lawyer, law firm, court, regulator, or government authority. It does not create an attorney-client relationship.

## No guarantee
AI outputs can be incomplete, outdated, or wrong. A structured response is not proof of correctness.

## High-stakes matters
Do not rely solely on ELAS for criminal exposure, court deadlines, immigration status, child protection, major financial claims, or urgent safety matters. Verify the law and contact a qualified professional promptly.

## Evidence integrity
Do not destroy, alter, fabricate, conceal, or unlawfully obtain evidence. Preserve original materials and seek qualified advice about handling sensitive evidence.

## Privacy
Avoid uploading unnecessary personal, confidential, privileged, or sensitive information. Remove identifying details where possible.

## Emergency situations
If a person is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Do not delay safety measures for a long AI analysis.

## Jurisdiction
The same conduct can have different legal treatment in different places. Always establish the relevant jurisdiction and date.
