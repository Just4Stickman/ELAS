# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is ELAS a legal service?
No. It is a prompt framework for general legal information and structured analysis.

## Does ELAS know the current law of every country?
No. It must use available sources and tools to verify current law. If verification is unavailable, it must say so.

## Does ELAS automatically know my country?
It should not assume jurisdiction. It asks which legal system applies when necessary.

## Can ELAS review contracts?
It can help identify clauses, obligations, risks, ambiguities, and questions for a qualified professional, subject to the completeness and quality of the supplied document.

## Can ELAS compare countries?
Yes, when requested and when enough information and reliable sources are available.

## Does a risk label predict a court outcome?
No. Risk labels are analytical categories, not probabilities or official legal findings.
