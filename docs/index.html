Introduction

A systematic approach to legal analysis

Legal questions often involve multiple jurisdictions, incomplete information, conflicting sources and complex interpretations. ELAS provides a structured methodology for examining these elements without presenting uncertainty as certainty.

01

Structured reasoning

Break complex legal questions into clearly defined analytical steps, relevant facts and legal issues.

02

Evidence-based conclusions

Distinguish established facts, assumptions, interpretations and unresolved uncertainties.

03

Transparent assessment

Explain the reasoning behind conclusions and identify the limitations of available information.

Core capabilities

What ELAS is designed to examine

The framework organizes legal analysis into distinct areas to support consistent, understandable and carefully qualified assessments.

Legal classification

Assess whether a described action appears lawful, unlawful or legally uncertain under the identified legal framework.

Jurisdiction identification

Identify the relevant country, legal system, territorial scope and potentially applicable international rules.

Legal basis

Identify relevant statutes, regulations, principles and authoritative legal sources where available.

Evidence assessment

Examine the reliability, relevance and completeness of the information supporting a legal assessment.

Risk identification

Highlight possible legal consequences, procedural concerns and relevant risk factors.

Uncertainty analysis

Identify missing facts, disputed interpretations and areas where a definitive conclusion cannot responsibly be reached.

Analytical methodology

From question to reasoned conclusion

ELAS follows a logical analytical structure that separates factual examination from legal interpretation and final assessment.

01

Define the question

Establish the relevant facts, legal question, parties and jurisdiction.

02

Examine the law

Identify applicable rules, legal principles and relevant authorities.

03

Apply and evaluate

Compare the facts with the legal requirements and assess competing interpretations.

04

Present the result

Explain the conclusion, legal basis, risks and remaining uncertainties.
