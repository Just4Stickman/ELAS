# Code of Conduct

## Our standard
Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful of different viewpoints.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Provide context and sources for legal claims.
- Disclose uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, and personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment or intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting fabricated legal authorities as genuine.
- Threats, abuse, or targeted discrimination.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation where necessary to maintain a safe and constructive project space.
