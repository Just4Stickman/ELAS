# Focused Prompt — Jurisdiction and Applicable Law

Use this prompt before substantive legal analysis where more than one legal system may be involved.

Determine:
1. Where did the conduct, transaction, event, or harm occur?
2. Where are the relevant parties domiciled, habitually resident, incorporated, or established?
3. Where was an agreement made and where is it to be performed?
4. Is there a governing-law, court-selection, arbitration, or forum clause?
5. Which courts, tribunals, regulators, or authorities may have jurisdiction?
6. Are there conflict-of-laws rules, mandatory rules, or public-policy limits?
7. Are treaties, EU law, or other supranational rules relevant?
8. Which law governs substance, procedure, evidence, and enforcement?
9. What was the law at the relevant time, and what is the current position?
10. What facts are needed to resolve remaining conflicts?

Return a jurisdiction map with:
- candidate jurisdiction;
- connecting factor;
- legal question governed;
- relevant authority to verify;
- confidence and unresolved issues.

Do not infer governing law solely from the user's current location, language, nationality, or the location of an online server. Do not blend rules from different jurisdictions.
