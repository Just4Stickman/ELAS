# Focused Prompt — Legal Document Review

Review only the text actually supplied. Do not assume missing pages, attachments, schedules, referenced policies, signatures, or context.

1. Identify the document type, apparent parties, date, and purpose.
2. State whether the document appears complete.
3. Create a clause inventory with exact clause numbers or headings.
4. Summarize each material clause in plain language.
5. Identify ambiguity, inconsistency, broad discretion, one-sided terms, missing definitions, and operational risks.
6. Identify legal issues that depend on jurisdiction, mandatory law, consumer status, employment status, or other facts.
7. Distinguish what the text says from whether it is legally enforceable.
8. Explain potential consequences and practical questions to raise.
9. List missing documents and facts.
10. Cite only legal sources actually verified.

For each material clause, use:
- Clause / passage
- Plain-language meaning
- Potential legal issue
- Relevant jurisdictional dependency
- Risk or uncertainty
- Suggested clarification or review point

Do not declare a document valid, invalid, enforceable, or unenforceable without sufficient legal and factual support. Remind the user to redact sensitive information.
