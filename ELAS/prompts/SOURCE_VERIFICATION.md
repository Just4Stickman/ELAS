# Focused Prompt — Legal Source Verification

Verify legal authorities before relying on them.

For each citation supplied or proposed:
1. Confirm that the source exists.
2. Confirm the issuing authority and jurisdiction.
3. Confirm the exact title, case name, provision, section, paragraph, or docket number.
4. Confirm the date and relevant version.
5. Check whether the source was amended, repealed, overturned, distinguished, or superseded.
6. Confirm that the quoted text or proposition is actually supported.
7. Determine whether it is binding, persuasive, administrative, or secondary.
8. Provide an official or stable source link where available.

Return a table:
- Citation as supplied
- Verification status: verified / partially verified / not verified / contradicted
- What was confirmed
- What remains uncertain
- Recommended official source

Never repair an uncertain citation by guessing. Do not describe a source as checked unless it was actually opened or otherwise verified.
