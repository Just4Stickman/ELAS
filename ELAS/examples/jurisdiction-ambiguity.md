# Example — Jurisdiction Ambiguity

**Fictional scenario:** A customer in Country A buys a digital service from a company established in Country B. The contract selects the law of Country C. The alleged harm occurs while the customer is travelling in Country D.

## Correct analytical approach

- Identify each connecting factor.
- Determine whether the governing-law clause is effective for the specific claim.
- Check mandatory consumer protections and any limits on choice of law.
- Separate contractual claims from statutory, regulatory, or tort claims.
- Determine which courts and regulators may have jurisdiction.
- Analyze applicable law separately from forum and enforcement.

## Common error to avoid

Do not simply apply the law named in the contract to every issue. Different claims may be governed by different rules, and mandatory protections may override parts of a contractual choice.
