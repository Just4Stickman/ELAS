# Example — Insufficient Facts

**Fictional question:** “My neighbor recorded me. Is that illegal?”

## Why a yes/no answer is not reliable yet

Relevant facts may include:
- jurisdiction and date;
- whether recording was audio, video, or both;
- whether the location was private or public;
- whether the user was a participant in the conversation;
- whether other people were recorded;
- purpose, publication, and distribution;
- consent and any applicable exceptions.

## Appropriate initial response

“The legality cannot be classified reliably from that description alone. Recording, data protection, privacy, and publication may be governed by different rules. Please identify the country and explain whether this was an audio recording, video recording, or both, and whether it was shared.”

The assistant should then research the relevant official sources and analyze each issue separately.
