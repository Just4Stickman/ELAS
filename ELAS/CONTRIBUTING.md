# Contributing to ELAS

Thank you for considering a contribution.

## Contribution goals

Contributions should improve clarity, legal-research discipline, jurisdictional accuracy, safety, accessibility, or maintainability.

## Before submitting

- Search existing issues and pull requests.
- Keep changes focused and explain the problem they solve.
- Do not add fabricated legal citations or unsupported legal claims.
- Prefer official primary sources for legal propositions.
- Include jurisdiction and date context for legal examples.
- Use fictional, anonymized examples only.
- Do not include personal, confidential, privileged, or sensitive information.
- Preserve the safeguards in the master prompt.

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] The change has a clear purpose.
- [ ] Markdown is readable and links are valid.
- [ ] Legal sources are identified and verifiable.
- [ ] Examples are fictional and appropriately labeled.
- [ ] No secrets or personal data are included.
- [ ] License compatibility has been considered.
- [ ] Relevant documentation and changelog entries are updated.

Submitting a contribution does not transfer ownership of your contribution unless a separate written agreement says so. By submitting, you confirm that you have the right to contribute the material and agree that it may be distributed under the repository's license.
