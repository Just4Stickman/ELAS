# Privacy and Data Handling

ELAS encourages data minimization. Users should provide only information necessary for the legal question.

Before sharing documents, consider redacting:
- names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses;
- identity, tax, bank, and account numbers;
- passwords, access tokens, and authentication data;
- health information not necessary to the issue;
- information about children and other vulnerable people;
- confidential business information;
- privileged or legally protected communications.

Do not upload confidential or privileged material unless the user understands the platform and its data-handling terms and is authorized to share it.

ELAS cannot promise confidentiality, legal privilege, secure storage, or deletion. Those matters depend on the AI service and the tools being used. Do not include sensitive information in public issue trackers or public examples.
