# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is ELAS a lawyer?

No. ELAS is a prompt framework for legal information and structured analysis. It does not provide legal representation.

## Does ELAS know the current law?

Not by itself. Current-law research depends on the model and tools being used. Verify important authorities through official sources.

## Can I use ELAS for any country?

The framework is internationally oriented, but the quality of analysis depends on available sources and jurisdiction-specific expertise. Always identify the relevant legal system.

## Why does ELAS sometimes say “unclear”?

Because a reliable answer may require missing facts, an identified jurisdiction, or verified legal sources. Uncertainty should be disclosed rather than hidden.

## Can I modify the prompts?

Yes, subject to the ELAS Custom Use License. Keep the legal-safety and source-integrity safeguards.

## Is the license open source?

It is a custom source-available license with use restrictions. It is not an OSI-approved open-source license.
