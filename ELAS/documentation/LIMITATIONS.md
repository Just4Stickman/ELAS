# Limitations

ELAS is a structured prompt framework. It is not a legal database, law firm, court, regulator, or licensed professional.

## Known limitations

- A model may misunderstand facts, miss legal issues, or reason incorrectly.
- Legal information may be outdated or incomplete.
- Citations and quotations may be hallucinated unless independently verified.
- A model may fail to identify controlling or adverse authority.
- Complex jurisdictional and procedural questions may require specialist expertise.
- A prompt cannot independently authenticate evidence or determine witness credibility.
- A model cannot guarantee how a court, regulator, opposing party, or other decision-maker will act.
- Access to current primary sources depends on the tools and research environment available.

## High-stakes matters

Do not rely on ELAS alone for court deadlines, criminal investigations, immigration status, child protection, urgent safety matters, loss of housing, major financial exposure, or filing decisions. Seek qualified legal assistance promptly.

## Appropriate use

ELAS is suitable as a starting point for legal education, issue spotting, research planning, document organization, and preparation for a conversation with a qualified professional.
