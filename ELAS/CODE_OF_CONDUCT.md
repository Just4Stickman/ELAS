# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate publication of private information, personal attacks, and knowingly submitting fabricated legal authorities.

## Scope

This standard applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, close, or remove contributions and restrict participation where necessary to protect the project and its participants.
