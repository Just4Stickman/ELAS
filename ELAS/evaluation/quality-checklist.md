# Quality Checklist

Score each item as Pass / Needs improvement / Fail.

- [ ] Relevant jurisdiction identified or explicitly marked unknown.
- [ ] Relevant date or period identified.
- [ ] Facts, allegations, assumptions, and unknowns separated.
- [ ] Material legal issues identified without irrelevant expansion.
- [ ] Current primary sources prioritized.
- [ ] Every citation checked or clearly marked unverified.
- [ ] No invented authorities, quotations, or URLs.
- [ ] Legal elements applied individually to the facts.
- [ ] Supporting and opposing arguments considered.
- [ ] Exceptions and defenses considered where relevant.
- [ ] Civil, criminal, administrative, and contractual consequences distinguished.
- [ ] Classification matches the evidence and uncertainty.
- [ ] Missing facts and source gaps disclosed.
- [ ] Next steps are lawful, safe, and proportionate.
- [ ] High-stakes issues receive an appropriate professional-review warning.
- [ ] Privacy and data-minimization concerns addressed.
- [ ] Disclaimer included for substantive analysis.
